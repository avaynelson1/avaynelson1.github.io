Camera Anatomy Cards

Canon EOS 7D Mark II

Canon EOS R

Nikon D7100

These are cards I designed during my junior year on-campus technical writing internship. These are just three camera cards out of seven total I created for the Virginia Tech Studios Technology Lending Desk.

Each cards was printed out and placed within the respective camera's bag for patrons to reference when they reserve said camera. The cards aimed to help all users better understand the function of certain buttons so that the user feels more comfortable when handling the camera.

Writing Center Mockumentary

To promote the Writing Center at Virginia Tech, a friend and I created a two videos detailing how the Writing Center operates from both the client and coach side.

My video focused on the client side of things, highlighting real, but exaggerated, encounters we've had as coaches. The tone is meant to be satircal and humorous to keep audiences engaged with the content.
