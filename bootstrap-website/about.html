Hi, I'm Ava!

I am currently an English Literature, Professional & Technical Writing, and Creative Writing major at Virginia Tech expecting to graduate in Spring of 2027. I've loved writing ever since I was a child.

At Tech, I've made the most of my education by joining numerous extracirriculars and taking on many employment roles. I've grown into my worth through my experience working as a coach the Writing Center, an editor at Silhouette Magazine and Studio Collective, and Vice President of Glossolalia: The Literary Festival at Virginia Tech. All of these roles, and so many more, have enabled me to expand my skillset beyond just writing and into more design- and technical-related fields.

Outside of writing, I love to play video games, bake, and ski! I love skiing so much I even joined the Virginia Tech Alpine Ski Team in my freshman year. Now, because of my involvement, I'm the official 2026-2027 ski team secretary!
